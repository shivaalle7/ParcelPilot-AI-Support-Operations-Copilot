# Product Note

## Additional client problem

I addressed Problem 1: Proactive Issue Detection.

The internal dashboard identifies:
- high-severity tickets
- tickets approaching SLA
- recurring issue categories
- unusual increases in complaint volume
- issues affecting multiple accounts

This turns the product from purely reactive Q&A into an operations intelligence layer.

## Priority roadmap

### P0
Reliable support agent, source-aware retrieval, authorization, confirmation.

### P1
Proactive issue detection and SLA monitoring.

### P2
Ticket workflow automation and customer follow-up tasks.

### P3
Production SSO/RBAC and audit trails.

### P4
Offline evaluation sets, feedback loops and continuous retrieval quality monitoring.

## Intentionally left out

- Real carrier API integrations
- Automatic refunds or credits
- Production SSO
- Autonomous irreversible actions
- Fine-tuning an LLM
- Complex multi-agent planning

These were excluded because they add security and operational risk without being necessary to demonstrate the assessment's core value.

## Primary usefulness metric

Correct Resolution Rate: the percentage of benchmark support requests answered correctly with appropriate evidence and without unnecessary escalation.
